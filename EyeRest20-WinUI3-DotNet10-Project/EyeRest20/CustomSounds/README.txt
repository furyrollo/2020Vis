EyeRest 20-20-20 - Custom Tones
===============================

There are two ways to use your own notification sounds:

1) EASIEST (no rebuild needed)
   In the running app, go to "Custom Tones" and click "Add Sound File..." to pick any
   audio file, or click "Open Custom Sounds Folder" and copy files into that folder,
   then click "Refresh List".

2) BUNDLE WITH THE APP
   Copy audio files into THIS folder (EyeRest20\CustomSounds) and rebuild/run the
   solution in Visual Studio. They are packaged with the app automatically.

Supported formats: .wav  .mp3  .m4a  .wma  .aac  .flac

Each file appears in the "Break Alert Sound" and "Resume Work Sound" lists as
"Custom: <file name>". Short sounds (1-5 seconds) work best.
